`fetch-lines.jsonl` is real output of `tools/yahoo/fetch.py` (yfinance 1.8.0) recorded on 2026-11-22 for nine symbols. It includes the London `GBp` quotes, where the market cap is in pence, and the two error lines Yahoo produced for `ROG.SW` and `ZZZZ`. The `ENEL.MI` line works on Yahoo, but the provider skips it because EODHD has no Milan exchange and `yahooSymbol` has no mapping for it.
